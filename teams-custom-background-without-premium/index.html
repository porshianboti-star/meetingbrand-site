Microsoft Teams backgrounds · IT how-to

Teams custom background without Premium

You do not need Teams Premium for a custom background in Microsoft Teams: any user can open Effects and avatars → Backgrounds → Add new and upload an image, unless their admin has turned custom images off. Teams Premium adds organization backgrounds that an admin uploads and requires centrally; without it, the MeetingBrand Agent places your company's branded background in each employee's Teams background gallery and they pick it once.

The procedure: one branded Teams background for everyone, without Teams Premium

  1. Create the background. Open meetingbrand.com/app/ and sign up with your work email. MeetingBrand fetches your logo and brand colors from that domain and renders 3 starter backgrounds; pick the room and the sign treatment the company should use. No Teams Premium licence is involved at any step.
  2. Add your people. My team → Upload users → upload a CSV with name, email, title and department. The Agent later matches each device to a person by the signed-in e-mail.
  3. Install the MeetingBrand Agent once. In My team, create an enrollment key (<ENROLLMENT_KEY>, per tenant) and copy the Intune (Windows) or Jamf (macOS) script; deploy it to your devices. It installs the Agent per user and schedules it every 4 hours.
  4. Check a pilot device. After Teams restarts, the Agent has written <GUID>.png and <GUID>_thumb.png into %LocalAppData%\Packages\MSTeams_8wekyb3d8bbwe\LocalCache\Microsoft\MSTeams\Backgrounds\Uploads (macOS: ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.teams2/Data/Library/Application Support/Microsoft/MSTeams/Backgrounds/Uploads), and My team shows the person as delivered.
  5. Each person selects it once. In Teams, open Effects and avatars → Backgrounds (Video effects in a meeting): the branded background is in the gallery. Select it once and Teams keeps it; nothing switches it for them.

What lands on each device: the script skeleton

New Teams lists a background only when <GUID>.png and a matching <GUID>_thumb.png sit in %LocalAppData%\Packages\MSTeams_8wekyb3d8bbwe\LocalCache\Microsoft\MSTeams\Backgrounds\Uploads (macOS: ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.teams2/Data/Library/Application Support/Microsoft/MSTeams/Backgrounds/Uploads). This is what the MeetingBrand Agent does there, redacted to a skeleton; the Intune or Jamf script generated in My team installs the Agent with your enrollment key, and the Agent runs this logic at logon and every 4 hours. Re-runs are idempotent: when the bytes already match, nothing is rewritten.

# What the MeetingBrand Agent does on each Windows device, as a PowerShell skeleton. The Intune or Jamf script generated in
# My team installs the Agent with your enrollment key; the Agent then runs this logic at logon and every 4 hours. Redacted: <...> are per tenant.
$OrgKey  = "<ENROLLMENT_KEY>"   # per tenant: MeetingBrand → My team → Agents → New enrollment key
$Uploads = "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Packages\MSTeams_8wekyb3d8bbwe\LocalCache\Microsoft\MSTeams\Backgrounds\Uploads"   # the folder new Teams reads
New-Item -ItemType Directory -Force -Path $Uploads | Out-Null
# one approved background = one stable GUID and TWO files; without the _thumb, new Teams does not list it
$Guid  = "<GUID>"
$Png   = Join-Path $Uploads "$Guid.png"            # the 1920×1080 export
$Thumb = Join-Path $Uploads "$($Guid)_thumb.png"   # 280×158 thumbnail
$Want  = "<SHA256>"                                # announced by MeetingBrand for this export
if ((Test-Path $Png) -and ((Get-FileHash $Png -Algorithm SHA256).Hash.ToLower() -eq $Want)) { exit 0 }   # idempotent: same bytes, nothing to do
Invoke-WebRequest -Uri "<SIGNED_EXPORT_URL>" -OutFile "$Png.tmp" -UseBasicParsing
if ((Get-FileHash "$Png.tmp" -Algorithm SHA256).Hash.ToLower() -ne $Want) { Remove-Item "$Png.tmp"; throw "sha256 mismatch" }
Move-Item -Force "$Png.tmp" $Png                   # atomic swap: a rebrand replaces the bytes under the same GUID
Invoke-WebRequest -Uri "<SIGNED_THUMB_URL>" -OutFile $Thumb -UseBasicParsing
# Restart Teams. The employee picks the background once under Video effects — no script can switch the active background.

What the employee sees

Nothing happens until Teams restarts. Then the branded background is one more tile under Effects and avatars → Backgrounds (Video effects in a meeting), with the thumbnail the Agent wrote next to it. The person picks it once and Teams keeps it for later meetings; nothing switches their active background for them, because no meeting platform lets a third party do that by API. A later rebrand replaces the image behind the same tile, so there is nothing to pick again. The Agent builds are unsigned developer builds for now, so a person who double-clicks the binary in a browser download would see Windows SmartScreen or macOS Gatekeeper; the scheduled task runs it without that prompt.

Teams Premium custom background: what Premium adds — and what it does not

Teams Premium lets an admin upload organization backgrounds in the Teams admin center and require them in meetings. Uploading your own custom image is part of standard Microsoft Teams and does not need Premium.

So for most companies the question is not whether a custom Teams background is possible. It is how every employee gets the same approved, branded image — without buying Teams Premium and without emailing a file around and hoping people upload it.

Questions

Can I use a custom background in Teams without Teams Premium?

Yes. Any Teams user can open Effects and avatars → Backgrounds → Add new and upload an image, unless their admin has turned custom images off. Teams Premium is needed only for admin-managed organization backgrounds.

What does Teams Premium add for backgrounds?

Organization backgrounds: an admin uploads approved images in the Teams admin center and can require them in meetings.

How does every employee get the same branded Teams background without Premium?

With the MeetingBrand Agent. IT installs it once with the Intune or Jamf script generated in MeetingBrand; it places the approved background in each person's Teams gallery, and each person picks it once. The Agent builds are unsigned developer builds for now, so Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper will prompt.

Does MeetingBrand switch the active Teams background for employees?

No. No meeting platform lets a third party switch a person's active background by API. The Agent places the image in the Teams gallery; the employee selects it once and Teams keeps it.

How do I deploy a custom Teams background to all users without Premium?

Put the image on each device. New Teams lists a background only when <GUID>.png and a matching <GUID>_thumb.png sit in %LocalAppData%\Packages\MSTeams_8wekyb3d8bbwe\LocalCache\Microsoft\MSTeams\Backgrounds\Uploads; the MeetingBrand Agent writes that pair on every Windows and Mac after IT deploys it once with the Intune or Jamf script generated in My team, and each person selects the background once. No Teams Premium licence is involved.

Why is there no option to add a custom background in Teams?

Usually because your organization's Teams meeting policy does not allow custom backgrounds (the video filters setting must allow all filters), or because you are under a Teams Premium required-background policy. Ask IT; once custom images are allowed, Add new appears under Effects and avatars → Backgrounds, and the MeetingBrand Agent can place the company background in that gallery for you.
