MeetingBrand documentation

How MeetingBrand delivers to each platform today, what is coming, what your employees see, and how to remove it. Written for the admin and for IT.

MeetingBrand is a web application that creates branded virtual backgrounds for video meetings from a company's website. An admin signs up with a work email; MeetingBrand fetches the company's public logo and brand colors from that domain, mounts the logo as a physical sign in any of 22 built-in office rooms, adds an optional name bar with each employee's name and title, and exports every background as a 1920×1080 image that works in Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Zoom and any other meeting app that accepts custom backgrounds. Delivery is built in for Microsoft Teams through the MeetingBrand Agent and for Google Meet through the MeetingBrand Chrome extension (today from the admin's own browser, and for every employee after the extension's Chrome Web Store listing); Zoom delivery and directory sync are coming. MeetingBrand is free during early access and is made by the team behind CompanyCard (company-card.com) and ProSignature (prosignature.co).

How do I get started with MeetingBrand?

Open the builder at meetingbrand.com/app/ and sign up with your work email; MeetingBrand fetches your company's public logo and brand colors from that domain and renders 3 starter backgrounds. Pick the rooms and sign treatments the company should use, add your people by CSV (name, email, title, department), then deliver: Microsoft Teams through the MeetingBrand Agent, Google Meet through the MeetingBrand Chrome extension, Zoom and any other meeting app as a 1920×1080 PNG download.

  1. Create MeetingBrand — open the builder, enter your company domain; review the logo, colors, scenes and sign treatments; set the name bar fields (name, title, department, company). Backgrounds are rendered in your browser; your brand kit and the backgrounds you save are stored with your account (see Where is the data stored below).
  2. Add your people — My team → Upload users → upload a CSV with name, email, title and department. Directory sync from Microsoft 365 (Entra ID), Google Workspace and Zoom is coming; when it opens, MeetingBrand imports your people (name, email, title, department) with the permissions listed below. Write to support@meetingbrand.com to be scheduled for early access.
  3. Deliver — Microsoft Teams: IT installs the MeetingBrand Agent with the Intune or Jamf script generated in My team (it carries your organization's enrollment key); each device is matched to a person by e-mail. Google Meet: the MeetingBrand Chrome extension, today from the admin's own browser, and for every employee after the extension's Chrome Web Store listing. Zoom and any other app: download the 1920×1080 PNG and set it once. My team shows each person's status: delivered, waiting for a restart, needs the employee's one click, or blocked (with the reason).

How does the MeetingBrand Agent deliver a Teams background without Teams Premium?

Microsoft Teams has no API for meeting backgrounds, so the MeetingBrand Agent — a small helper for Windows and macOS that IT installs once with the Intune or Jamf script generated in My team — writes the approved 1920×1080 background into the Teams uploads folder on each device as <GUID>.png with a <GUID>_thumb.png next to it. After Teams restarts the image is under Effects and avatars → Backgrounds; the employee selects it once and Teams keeps it, and a later rebrand swaps the bytes under the same GUID. No Teams Premium licence is involved, and nothing switches anyone's active background.

How do I deploy it with Intune (Windows) or Jamf (macOS)?

In My team, generate the Intune (Windows) or Jamf (macOS) script; it carries your organization's enrollment key. Intune: Devices → Scripts → Windows 10 and later, run using the logged-on credentials, 64-bit PowerShell. Jamf: a policy that runs the shell script as the console user. The script downloads the Agent, verifies its sha256, registers a per-user scheduled task (Windows) or LaunchAgent (macOS) that runs it at logon and every 4 hours, and runs it once; the Agent then matches the device to a person by the signed-in e-mail and writes the approved background into the Teams uploads folder. Pilot with a small group first: the builds are unsigned developer builds for now.

Before you roll it out: the user's Teams meeting policy must allow custom backgrounds (video filters set to all filters), and a user under a Teams Premium required-background policy belongs on the Premium track instead. macOS 27 needs an MDM privacy (PPPC) Full Disk Access profile for the Agent, or the write into the Teams container is reported as blocked in My team.

Removing or re-running: re-runs are idempotent (same bytes, nothing rewritten). To remove, see How do I remove MeetingBrand from a device.

What happens in Zoom today?

Today each person downloads the 1920×1080 PNG from MeetingBrand and adds it once under Zoom Settings → Video & effects → Virtual backgrounds; Zoom remembers it. The Zoom integration — uploading the background into each employee's own Zoom background library — is built and awaits approval of its Zoom App Marketplace app.

Status, November 3, 2026: Coming. The Zoom integration — uploading the background into each employee's own Zoom background library — is built and awaits approval of its Zoom App Marketplace app. Today each person downloads the 1920×1080 PNG and sets it once in Zoom.

When the Zoom integration opens — permissions it will request (admin-managed app): list users; upload and delete virtual background files for users; read and update account settings related to virtual backgrounds. A Zoom account owner or admin authorizes once for the whole account; employees see no prompt.

What will happen then: each active employee's branded background is uploaded into their own Zoom virtual-background library (Zoom allows up to 10 files per user; we manage ours). After the upload the employee signs out and back in to Zoom, opens Settings → Video & effects → Virtual backgrounds and picks the branded image once — Zoom remembers it. Admins can additionally turn on "Require users to always use virtual background" and disable personal uploads so the branded image is the only non-stock choice.

How do I remove MeetingBrand from a device?

Microsoft Teams: remove the Agent from Add/Remove programs (Windows) or the Applications folder (macOS) and revoke the device in My team; the Agent deletes only the <GUID>.png and <GUID>_thumb.png pairs it wrote itself. Google Meet: remove the extension from Chrome, or from the force-install list in the Google Admin console once it is managed. Zoom: nothing is installed today; delete the PNG under Zoom Settings → Video & effects → Virtual backgrounds. For your whole workspace, write to support@meetingbrand.com and we delete it.

  • Microsoft Teams: remove the Agent from Add/Remove programs (Windows) or the Applications folder (macOS), and revoke the device in My team. Once directory sync is connected: Entra admin center → Enterprise applications → MeetingBrand → Delete (revokes our access).
  • Google Meet: remove the extension from Chrome (or, once force-installed, from the Chrome force-install list in the Google Admin console). Once directory sync is connected: Google Admin → Security → API controls → Manage third-party app access → MeetingBrand → Remove.
  • Zoom (once the integration is added): Zoom App Marketplace → Manage → Added Apps → MeetingBrand → Remove. MeetingBrand deletes the background files it uploaded and the directory data it synced for that account within 30 days (immediately on request to support@meetingbrand.com).
